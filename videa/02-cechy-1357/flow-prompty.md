# Prompty pro obrázky (Nano Banana) a animace (Flow) – Přežil bys ve středověkých Čechách? Rok 1357

Celkem 42 obrázků, z toho **12 animovat ve Flow (Veo)**; zbytek rozpohybujeme v Remotionu (zoom, pan, parallax).

## 1. Instrukce pro Nano Banana (jednou na začátku konverzace / do Gemu)

Přilož výřez postavy `videa/postava/postava-zepredu.png` a pošli tento text. Pak už posílej jen prompty scén.

```text
You are my illustrator for a Czech faceless YouTube channel with long-form animated explainers ("What if you travelled back in time…", "How long would you survive…"). For every scene I send, you create ONE image in 16:9 landscape format. All images of a video must look like frames from the same animated film.

THE MAIN CHARACTER IS IN THE ATTACHED IMAGE. Keep him exactly as he is drawn there in every image: the same large perfectly round white head (about one third of his height) with a thin black outline, the same two black dot eyes and small simple mouth, no nose, ears or hair, the same bright orange hoodie with hood, drawstrings and kangaroo pocket, the same dark blue jeans, white sneakers and round white mitten-like hands. Do not redesign, restyle or recolor him. You may change only his pose, facial expression (simple eyebrows and mouth, like in the reference) and what he holds. He is the only person in modern clothes and the only bright orange thing in the picture.

Every other person is drawn the same way as him: large round white head, dot eyes, simple mouth, white mitten hands and a clothed body with thin black outlines, but dressed in clothing of the scene's era in muted colors. Never draw realistic people and never the likeness of a real, recognizable person.

The look is a 2D hand-drawn explainer illustration: flat-colored cartoon characters placed in richly detailed painted backgrounds, a muted palette matching the era of the scene, soft natural light and a subtle paper texture. Architecture, vehicles, clothing and objects are accurate for the place and year of the scene; nothing modern appears except what the main character carries. The composition is cinematic and the bottom part of the frame stays calm because subtitles will go there.

Never put any text, letters, numbers, readable signs, logos or watermarks into the image; titles are added later in editing.

When I send a scene, reply only with the image. If I write "again", generate a new version of the same scene. If I write "fix: …", edit the last image only as described and keep everything else unchanged.
```

## 2. Scény

Každý prompt je samostatný – funguje i bez instrukcí výše, když k němu přiložíš výřez postavy.

### S01 · Úvod · 🎬 VEO

> Minule jsi stál na Karlově mostě v roce 1926. Pak přišel záblesk. A teď ležíš v blátě, kolem tebe pobíhají slepice a nad tebou se sklání chlap s vidlemi.

**Obrázek:**

```text
Create a 16:9 landscape illustration. Use the orange-hoodie character from the attached image and keep him exactly as drawn: same round white head, face, hoodie, jeans, sneakers and proportions. Scene: A muddy medieval Bohemian village with wooden thatched houses and a castle on a hill in the background. The orange-hoodie character from the attached image lies on his back in the mud in the foreground, medium-large in the frame, looking up dazed; a round-headed cartoon peasant in a simple tunic leans over him holding a wooden pitchfork; a few chickens stand around them. Draw all other people in the same cartoon style: large round white heads, dot eyes, white mitten hands, period clothing in muted colors. Style: 2D hand-drawn explainer illustration, flat-colored characters in a richly detailed painted background, muted earthy medieval palette of ochre, moss green, warm brown and soft teal, soft natural light, subtle paper texture. No text, letters, numbers or logos anywhere in the image.
```

**Animace (Flow → Frames to Video, start frame = obrázek výše):**

```text
2D hand-drawn cartoon animation. Keep the exact art style, colors, line work and composition of the start frame; flat illustrated look, no 3D, no realism, no new objects or people.

Camera: completely static, locked-off shot, no pan, no zoom.

Action, in this order:
0–3 s: the chickens around him flap their wings and hop a short distance away.
3–6 s: the orange-hoodie character sits up slowly in the mud and blinks.
6–8 s: the peasant with the pitchfork leans a little closer and frowns.

The houses, the castle and the sky stay still and keep their exact shape. The orange-hoodie character keeps his round white head, dot eyes, orange hoodie, jeans and white sneakers exactly as in the start frame.

Duration 8 seconds. Audio: chickens clucking and a squelch of mud, no music, no speech.
```
